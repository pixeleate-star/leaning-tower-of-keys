# Leaning Key Organizer

A slanting key holder for my wall and yes the **lean** is on purpose

It is a big hexagon shaped organizer inspired by the Leaning Tower of Pisa
Five pegs for keys with KEYS embossed right under them in case my keys forget where home is
A pocket tray sticking out from the wall for wallet phone and coins
And a ribbed back panel above it so the whole thing does not look like a boring slab

Size is 37.9 cm x 40 cm and it is made for one exact wall in my room

## Why I Made This

Every time I came home my keys wallet and random pocket stuff landed on whatever surface was closest
Next morning I was searching the whole room like a detective
I wanted one fixed spot right next to the door where everything goes
I also did not want a tiny generic holder from the internet
So I measured my wall and designed this to fit that exact space
It looks like it belongs there instead of something stuck on later
Then came the fun part
Looking at the top and bottom edges you will see they are slightly tilted
That is the Pisa inspiration
A little imperfection that is totally planned and honestly makes it way cooler than a straight rectangle

## What Makes It Unique

- **The Pisa tilt** Most wall organizers are perfect rectangles or circles and this one leans on purpose so it has personality even when it is empty
- **Three ways to hang it** The back has one sawtooth hanger slot whose teeth let me adjust it and get it level on the wall plus two keyhole slots as backup so if I mess up a hole I do not reprint the whole thing and waste filament money
- **Made for my wall** At 37.9 cm x 40 cm it is way bigger than a normal key holder and that is the point because I sized it to the space I actually have
- **Keys and pocket tray in one piece** Pegs and tray live on the same part so everything I empty from my pockets goes to one spot instead of two separate holders
- **Ribbed back panel** The vertical grooves above the tray break up the big surface and give it a clean textured look
